Blurb

Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Blurb lets you record short voice notes (“blurbs”) and reminds you to come back to them. This policy explains what Blurb collects, why, who helps run it, and your rights.

Who we are

Blurb is made by Ifemide Cole, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for your data (the “controller” under UK data protection law). Contact: ifemidecole@gmail.com.

What Blurb collects

  • Your account: your name and email address from Apple or Google when you sign in. If you choose “Hide My Email” with Apple, we only get Apple’s private relay address.
  • Your blurbs: your recordings, the text transcribed from them, any names you give them, and when you made them.
  • Reminders and check-ins: when you’ve asked to be reminded, and your answers to “Still on your mind?”.
  • Your phone’s notification address: a code that lets us send your reminders to your phone.

Blurb does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, doesn’t sell your data, and doesn’t share it with anyone except the services below that help run the app.

Why we use it

Only to run Blurb for you: to sign you in, keep your blurbs, turn them into text, and send the reminders you ask for. The legal basis is that it’s needed to provide the service you signed up for.

Who helps run Blurb

  • Supabase stores your account, blurbs and recordings. Your data is stored in Ireland (EU).
  • OpenAI turns your recordings into text. OpenAI says it doesn’t keep audio sent for transcription and doesn’t use it to train its models.
  • Expo and Apple deliver your reminder notifications. A notification includes your blurb’s name or its first line, so that text passes through their notification services.
  • Apple and Google handle signing in.

Some of these companies are based in the US, so your data may be handled there. When that happens it’s protected by the safeguards UK law requires.

On your phone

A recording is kept on your phone until it has uploaded, then the phone’s copy is removed. If you sign out before a blurb uploads, it’s removed from the phone (Blurb warns you first).

How long we keep things

  • Your blurbs are kept until you delete them.
  • A deleted blurb goes to Recently deleted, where you can restore it for 30 days. After that, it and its recording are permanently removed.
  • If you delete your account, Blurb immediately removes its access to your Apple or Google sign-in and stops sending you notifications. If you sign back in within 30 days, you can keep your account. After 30 days, your account, blurbs and recordings are permanently removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, or object to how it’s used. Most of this you can do in the app: rename or delete blurbs, or delete your account in Settings. For anything else, email ifemidecole@gmail.com and you’ll get a reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Blurb isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above, and we’ll let you know before important changes take effect.
