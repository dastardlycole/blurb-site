Blurb

Support

Blurb lets you say what’s on your mind as a short recording (a “blurb”), turns it into text, and reminds you to come back to it later.

Get in touch

Something not working, or an idea? Email ifemidecole@gmail.com. It helps to say which phone you use and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Blurb can’t hear me

Blurb asks to use your microphone the first time you tap record. If you said no, open your phone’s Settings app, find Blurb, and turn on Microphone. You can see whether it’s on in Blurb’s own Settings too.

My reminders don’t arrive

Reminders are notifications, so Blurb needs permission to send them. Check Notifications in Blurb’s Settings. If it says Off, tap it to open your phone’s settings for Blurb and turn notifications on.

I recorded without a connection

Your blurb is saved on your phone straight away and uploads when you’re back online. The text appears shortly after it uploads.

The text of my blurb is missing

The text usually appears a few seconds after a blurb uploads. If it says “Couldn’t transcribe this one”, the recording is still saved and plays as normal. Email us if it keeps happening.

How long can a blurb be?

Up to 15 minutes. Blurb warns you shortly before the limit, then saves the blurb when you reach it.

I deleted a blurb by mistake

Deleted blurbs go to Recently deleted in Settings for 30 days. Tap Restore to bring one back. After 30 days it’s gone for good.

How do I delete my account?

In Blurb, open Settings and tap Delete account. Your account and everything in it are permanently removed after 30 days. If you change your mind, sign back in before then to keep it.
